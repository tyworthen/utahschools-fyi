How Utah school property taxes work

Your property tax bill is the sum of several separate taxes — county, city, special districts, and the school district. The school district portion is usually the largest single piece.

The basic calculation is:

Calculation

Taxable value × school tax rate = school district tax

For a primary residence, taxable value is 55% of market value, because Utah exempts 45% of a primary home’s value.

But the “school tax rate” is not one number. It is several separate levies added together, each with its own legal purpose and rules.

Truth in Taxation

Each year, the state calculates a certified tax rate for the district: the rate that would bring in the same property tax revenue as last year, not counting new construction. When property values rise, the certified rate falls.

If the district wants to collect more than that, it must advertise a proposed tax increase and hold a public hearing before adopting a higher rate. Those hearings are a direct opportunity for taxpayers to ask questions.
