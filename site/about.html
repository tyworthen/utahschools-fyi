Independent data for informed taxpayers.

Independent, nonpartisan research and analysis of Tooele County School District finances, taxation, spending, enrollment, staffing, debt, and educational outcomes.

Our mission

To give Tooele County taxpayers an independent, factual, and accessible analysis of how their school district raises, spends, and manages public money.

  1. Government accountability
  2. Taxpayer education
  3. Financial transparency
  4. Investigation of spending that appears inefficient, unusual, or unexplained

Our goal is not to tell you what to conclude. It is to give you enough reliable information to reach your own conclusions.

Who this is for

Tooele County taxpayers first — and also parents, educators, school employees, community organizations, journalists, researchers, and public officials. We write for intelligent readers who may not know government accounting terms.

Political neutrality

✓ We do

  • Investigate government spending
  • Identify unusual trends
  • Compare districts
  • Question unexplained spending
  • Analyze tax burdens
  • Publish evidence, including evidence unfavorable to the district

✕ We don’t

  • Support or oppose candidates or parties
  • Take positions on bonds, tax increases, or ballot measures
  • Assume misconduct or infer intent
  • Call spending wasteful without evidence
  • Use inflammatory language
Independence

This project is independent of Tooele County School District and is not affiliated with the district or the Utah State Board of Education.
