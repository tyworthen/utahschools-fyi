Compensation: a deep dive

Salaries are the largest part of a school district’s budget. This page follows every Tooele County School District employee’s pay from FY2019 to FY2026, as reported to the State Auditor, and compares it with Iron, Cache, and Box Elder. It covers total payroll, teacher pay, administrative staffing, who earns $100,000 or more, and how pay is coded.

    How pay is coded

    Utah districts report salaries to the state by account code (for example, 131 for teachers or 115 for supervisors and directors). Comparing Tooele’s pay records with its own staff directory and with the state’s financial reports shows several places where the codes do not match what the job appears to be. None of these show money was misspent. They matter because comparisons across districts, including some on this page, rely on the codes.

      Nineteen district-level staff, including coordinators and specialists, are paid from the teacher salary account, and maintenance and grounds wages are reported under technology. How does the district decide which account a position is charged to, and do these choices affect the instruction and administration figures reported to the state?

      What we don’t know

      • Full-time status. The data has no hours or FTE. “Full-time” here means paid at least $40,000 in FY2026 dollars, which leaves out part-time and partial-year employees but may include some people paid for extra assignments.
      • Why positions were added. Pay records show how many people were paid from each account, not why positions were created or what they do. Board budget documents and job descriptions would answer that.
      • Matching people across years. The records have no employee ID, and name formats change between years. People are matched on first name and last surname, so a few may be merged or split.
      • Benefits.
      • Cache after FY2021. Cache stopped reporting state account codes in FY2022, so its roles cannot be compared after FY2021, and its FY2026 file appears incomplete.
      • Contracted staff. Work done by contractors, such as substitute teachers hired through an agency, is paid as purchased services and does not appear in payroll.

      Method and data

      How these figures are calculated

      People and roles. Transparent Utah lists each employee’s pay as separate lines by fund, school or department, and account. Lines are added up for each person and year. A person’s role is the account that carries most of their salary: 11x district administration, 12x school administration, 131 teachers, 14x other licensed staff (counselors, psychologists, nurses, specialists), 15x office, 16x aides, 17x transportation, 18x maintenance, custodial, and technology, and 19x food service, coaches, and other.

      Full-time and medians. “Full-time” = salary of at least $40,000 in FY2026 dollars, adjusted for inflation each year so that raises alone do not move people across the line. Medians for groups of fewer than ten people are not shown, so no individual’s pay can be inferred.

      Per student. Salaries ÷ October 1 enrollment minus online-school enrollment. Tooele’s FY2021 rise reflects about 1,650 students moving into its online school during COVID while staff stayed on payroll.

      Central vs. school. Based on each district’s own location labels in the pay records (for example, “030 DISTRICT OFFICE” in Tooele or “District Level” in Iron), so treat cross-district differences as approximate.

      Salaries only. Dollar amounts are salaries and wages; benefits are excluded except where stated.
