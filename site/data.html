Standardized figures for all 41 Utah school districts, FY2012–FY2025. Filter, check our work, and download. Blank cells are values not available in the source — never estimates.

Downloads

Normalized datasets are long-format CSVs keyed by USBE district number (lea_number), generated by scripts/build_usbe_data.py from the preserved original files.

revenue.csv

Local, state, federal, total, and property-tax revenue by district and year.

expenditures.csv

Spending by category (instruction, administration, …) by district and year.

enrollment.csv

October 1 enrollment by district and school year, FY2015–FY2027, with online-program enrollment for Tooele and Iron.

budgets.csv

General Fund original budget, final budget, and actual expenditures, FY2013–FY2024.

fund_balances.csv

General Fund year-end balance by component.

validation_FY2024.csv

Where USBE’s two FY2024 sources disagree by more than 0.5%.

source_registry.csv

Every acquired source: authority, URL, coverage, retrieval date, checksum.
