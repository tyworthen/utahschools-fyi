# Transparent Utah — all-employee compensation, district summary

Generated by `scripts/build_compensation_data.py` from the State Auditor's Compensation Downloader
(https://transparent.utah.gov/entities/compensation-downloader, FY2019–FY2026 for Tooele, Iron, Cache, and
Box Elder, retrieved 2026-11-07), with a cross-check against the Tooele County School District staff directory.

The downloads list individual employees by name, so they are kept outside the published site (`private_data/`,
excluded from git) and never published. This folder contains only district-level figures: total salaries and
benefits, salaries per brick-and-mortar student, full-time headcounts and median salaries by role, students per
administrator and per teacher, employees paid $100,000 or more, and teacher retention. Medians for groups of
fewer than 10 people are left blank.

`compensation_by_district.csv` is long format: `district, fiscal_year, metric, value`. Definitions are in the
script's docstring and on https://utahschools.fyi/tooele-compensation under "Method and data".
