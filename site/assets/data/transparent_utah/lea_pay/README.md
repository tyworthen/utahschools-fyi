# Transparent Utah — Administrator & Teacher Pay, by district

From https://transparent.utah.gov/lea-pay/ (Office of the Utah State Auditor), LEA Specific tab,
downloaded one district at a time with "Download as CSV", retrieved 2026-10-31. One file per school
district (`lea_pay_<USBE district number>.csv`), FY2014-FY2025 where reported.

Columns: fiscal_year, lea_name, job_category (Administrator, Teacher, Student Support, Operations &
Facilities, Extracurricular, Other Staff), num_employees, total_amount, cat_median, cat_average
(total compensation: salary + benefits, all employees including part-time).

Aggregated by the Auditor; contains no individual records. Raw, unaudited. Job categories are
assigned from job titles and account codes, so they are not perfectly comparable across districts.
The site uses the Administrator category only; teacher counts here are not reliable headcounts.
