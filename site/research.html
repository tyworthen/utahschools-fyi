Areas of investigation

Our standing research agenda. We look for rapid growth, large differences from peers, unexplained changes, unusual transfers, persistent budget variances, and differences between public statements and financial records.

How every investigation is written

Each published analysis follows the same structure, so readers always know where to find the evidence, the limits, and the sources.

  1. Headline — clear and factual
  2. Executive summary
  3. What we found
  4. The numbers
  5. Why it matters
  6. How Tooele compares
  7. What we don’t know
  8. Possible explanations
  9. Sources, methodology & data download

We don’t write

“District wastes millions”

We write

“Administrative spending increased X% over ten years. What changed?”
