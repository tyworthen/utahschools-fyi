Scope and limits

School districts only; charter schools are excluded until their figures can be shown with a clear district/charter distinction. All dollars are nominal. Per-student and inflation-adjusted figures will follow once enrollment and CPI data are added. FY2012–FY2024 come from USBE’s Tableau Public extracts and FY2025 from USBE’s Excel summaries; the two agree for FY2024 in 162 of 164 district-level checks (see validation).
