Then:

```text

        ↓
CPI adjustment
        ↓
2026 dollars
        ↓
real spending trend
```

## The first dataset I'd build

If you're ready to start collecting data, I would **not** start with PDFs.

I'd start with these five:

1. **Transparent Utah expenditure data** — FY2014–2025
2. **Transparent Utah revenue data** — FY2014–2025
3. **Transparent Utah compensation data** — FY2014–2025
4. **USBE enrollment/outcome data**
5. **NCES F-33 finance data**

Then use TCSD's actual budgets/audits to **validate and enrich** the standardized datasets.

That gives you a very powerful first analytical dataset:

> **12 years × 4 districts × revenue + spending + staffing + enrollment + outcomes**

From there, we can start answering questions such as:

- Spending per student vs. peer districts
- Spending growth vs. enrollment growth
- Administrative spending growth
- Compensation growth vs. inflation
- Revenue growth by source
- Property-tax growth
- Instructional spending as a percentage of total spending
- Debt per student
- Spending vs. graduation/achievement outcomes

And importantly, we can identify **the anomalies worth investigating**, rather than deciding in advance what the answer should be.
